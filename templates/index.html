Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator really free?

Yes. You can create unlimited QR codes at no cost. There are no watermarks or hidden fees.

Will my QR codes expire?

No. Once you download a QR code it is yours forever. Just make sure the destination URL stays live.

What devices can scan my QR codes?

All modern smartphones with cameras can scan QR codes. Both iPhone (iOS 11+) and Android devices have built-in QR code readers in their camera apps. No special app required.

What file format do I get when I download?

Your QR code downloads as a high-quality PNG image file. This format works perfectly for both digital sharing and professional printing.

Can I use QR codes for commercial purposes?

Absolutely! Use them on business cards, product packaging, advertisements, restaurant menus, event tickets, or any marketing materials. There are no usage restrictions.

How do I make sure my QR code works?

After downloading, simply scan it with your phone's camera to verify it directs to the correct URL. Test on both iPhone and Android devices before printing in bulk.

What types of URLs can I convert to QR codes?

Any valid URL works: websites, YouTube videos, Google Forms, PDF documents, social media profiles, Google Maps locations, and more. Just paste the link and generate.

Do I need to create an account?

No account needed. Simply enter your URL and download your QR code instantly. We don't store your data or require registration.

Can I track scans or customize colors?

This version focuses on fast, private QR code creation. Advanced features like analytics or custom branding are on the roadmap — keep an eye on future updates.

How big should I print my QR code?

For reliable scanning, print QR codes at least 2x2 cm (0.8x0.8 inches). Larger is better for distant scanning. The PNG file scales perfectly to any size.

Are QR codes secure?

QR codes themselves are just links encoded visually. Always scan codes from trusted sources. This generator creates standard QR codes with no malicious code or tracking.

Can I edit a QR code after creating it?

Static QR codes (like ours) cannot be edited once created. If you need to change the destination URL, simply generate a new QR code with the updated link.
